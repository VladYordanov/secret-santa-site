Privacy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Secret Santa: Pass the Phone does not collect any data.

What stays on your phone

The names, dates, budgets and matches you enter are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting a draw, or the app, deletes them.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or email addresses.
  • No servers: the app works fully offline.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • No data is shared with anyone.
  • No in-app purchases.

Widgets and Lock Screen

The countdown widget and Lock Screen countdown read the same on-device data to show the days until your exchange. Nothing leaves the phone.

Sharing

If you tap Share, iOS shares a link to the App Store using the app you choose. We don't see what you share.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
