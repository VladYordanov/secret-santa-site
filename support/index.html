How does a draw work?

Add the people in the room. You can keep a couple apart, note a budget, and set the day you exchange gifts. Then start the draw.

How do we pass the phone?

Each person taps their own name, checks that only they can see the screen, and opens their envelope. After they seal it, they pass the phone to the next person.

How do I add a lot of names?

Copy your list from Notes, Messages or a spreadsheet, then tap Paste list on the People step. Names can be separated by commas, new lines, semicolons, "&" or "and".

How do I keep couples apart?

On the People step, tap More, pick two names and tap Keep apart.

How do I redraw?

Open the draw and tap Redraw. Everyone gets a new name, so confirm with the group first.

How do I delete a group?

On the draw list, swipe the group and tap Delete. That removes it from the phone.

Someone forgot who they got.

Open the event and press and hold the eye icon next to their name.

Someone tapped the wrong name.

Tap Not me on the hand-off screen. Nothing is revealed. If the envelope was already opened, use Redraw to start fresh.

Does it need internet or an account?

No. Everything works offline and stays on your phone.
